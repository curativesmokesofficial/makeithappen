## ABSOLUTE STEERING INDEX (ASI) - AROYA
Scale 1-10: 10 = max vegetative, 1 = max generative
- ASI 1 (max gen): Irrigate to field capacity first event, no second window
- ASI 10 (max veg): First event to field capacity + second window maintaining field capacity all day
- ASI 2-9: Adjust second window duration
With AROYA AUTOMAIT: Set ASI goal, AI monitors sensors and triggers exact irrigation automatically - RECOMMENDED for precision

## IRRIGATION AT A GLANCE (Golden Rule)
- Veg: frequent, small shots, small dryback
- Week 1-3 Flower: infrequent, large shots, large dryback (generative)
- Week 4-6 Flower: frequent, small shots, small dryback (vegetative - bulking)
- Week 7-9 Flower: infrequent, large shots, large dryback (generative - ripening)

## VEGETATIVE SIGNALS (Stems + Leaves Growth)
- Shot size: 2-4%
- Frequency: Every 15-40 min
- Dryback: 10-20% = 25% of max field capacity. Example max WC 60% -> veg dryback to 45% (min WC 45%)
- Intershot dryback: 1-4%
- VPD: 0.9-1.0
- Graph has P1 (rehydration) + P2 (maintenance)
- Math Example: 6 events x 3% = 18% total shots - 5 intershot drybacks x 1% = 5% = 13% P1 rehydration

## GENERATIVE SIGNALS (Flower + Fruit Growth)
- Shot size: Larger (5%+)
- Frequency: Infrequent, large gaps
- Dryback: 50% of max field capacity. Example max WC 60% -> gen dryback to 30%
- P1 only for Weeks 1-3 flower (no P2)
- Intershot: Larger
- Goal: Build EC, stack, create stress for flower initiation

## SUBSTRATE GRAPH PHASES (Critical)
- Phase 1 Rehydration: Daily irrigation start, bring to Max Field Capacity
- Phase 2 Maintenance: Reach max WC and equal dryback/irrigation period (Monitoring Phase) - P2 shots control dryback
- Phase 3 Dryback: Lights out drying period (Overnight) - From Stop to Start
- Irrigation Window = first to last event of day
- Max Field Capacity + Water Limit lines
- Every genetic unique uptake rate - data crucial

P2 LOGIC:
- Look at previous day WC% per hour. If WC drops 2% per hour -> give 2% P2 maintenance shot each hour (vegetative)
- If drops 4% over 2 hours -> 4% P2 (generative maintenance)
- To adjust dryback: If you have 17% dryback and want 15%, add P2 based on day before so WC only dries back 15%
- Slow genetics (Mac 1, Kush Mints): Only use P1 events (drink slower)

## ENVIRONMENTAL SETPOINTS
Veg: Day & Night 80F, 72% RH, VPD 0.9
Week 1-3 Flower: Day & Night 80F, 70% RH, VPD 1.0
Week 4-7 Flower: Day 80F, 67% RH, 1.1 VPD | Night 72F, 55% RH, 1.1 VPD
Week 8-9 Flower: Day 70F, 50% RH, 1.2 VPD | Night 60F, 40% RH, 1.1 VPD
Optional bulking/ripening VPD experiment: 1.2-1.3 (lower EC as VPD rises, move 0.1 at a time)

Leaf Surface Temp:
- LED: 2-5° less than ambient
- HPS: 1-4° less than ambient
Check apex in full light, 2 hours after lights on, daily

- Same shot size spread further = less runoff = build EC

## RUNOFF COLLECTION (Critical Daily Priority)
Why: Substrate sensors don't tell volume or pH. Graphs can look good but no runoff = deficiencies over time, nutrient ratio imbalance. Tracks system issues (clogged lines, EC higher than tank)

Manual Method:
- Rockwool slabs: Prop slab on 2 quad thick trays, one at end to catch runoff
- Coco: Cube on riser inside saucer/tray
- Add extra emitter taped to 2,000ml measuring cup = input water
- Wait 24 hours, collect same time daily (e.g., 9am)
- Pour tray water into 2,000ml cup, record volume, pH, EC in spreadsheet (free at ccibook.com members)
- Input collection x number of emitters = total input
- Runoff % = Runoff Volume / Total Input Volume
Example: Input pitcher 500ml x 2 emitters =1000ml total, Runoff 500ml = 50% runoff
Example 2: 500ml x 3 emitters =1500ml, Runoff 500ml =33%

Automated: AROYA Drip and Drain station - continuous volume + EC data, syncs to dashboards, avoids manual entry, waste less water/nutrients, course correct real-time

## ROOTING IN PROCESS
Avoid overwater at transplant:
- Rooting In: Follow CCI guidelines, clones never overwatered
Flower Overwater Fix (Week 1-3):
1. Dryback substrate to 25% - may mean no water for day or 6 hours after lights on
2. Use substrate sensor (AROYA Solus or TEROS ONE) to verify 25%
3. Once dryback reached, feed to get back on normal schedule (first feed 2 hours after lights on normally)
4. Example: Lights on 12pm, normal first feed 2pm 6x200ml, but dryback delayed to 6pm - feed only 3-4 events to hit 25% dryback next day by 2pm
5. Once schedule corrected, hydrate fully + get 5-15% runoff
6. Check EC/pH - EC low = couple days no runoff to build, pH off = adjust batch tank opposite (pH 5.0 runoff -> feed 6.5 next day)
7. Collect data, observe - eagle claw from overwater goes away 4-5 days if dried correctly

Low VPD Fix: Can't lower humidity -> raise temp couple degrees to raise VPD. If max temp reached + low VPD -> feed less to avoid overwater.

Experiment: Double emitters on one plant or extra pitcher daily to see overwater look for that genetic.

Food for Thought: Multiple events, high WC%, small drybacks = vegetative signals = rapid growth. During bulking = great for yield. During stretch = huge stalks, stretch, extra leaves. Timing everything.

## DATA COLLECTION + OBSERVATION (Most Important)
Collecting data = single most important part. Allows replicate best run, track adjustments, not guessing.
What to Track:
- Plant Height: Every 2-3 days veg through day 21-28 (until stretch stops)
- Irrigation: Log every event down to minute - AROYA helps with duration/volume
- Runoff: Daily priority - volume, pH, EC + input water tracking
- Yield per sqft: Adjust density, environment, irrigation
- Photos: 2-3x per week to compare runs

Signs:
- Underwater / Too high EC: Short plants, fan leaves yellow/burn, flower sets early tight unhealthy -> may need add P2 events weeks 1-3
- Overwater: Stretch more than desired, multiple inch
